## Fixed after the review
1. **(blocker) `exec.mjs` — a solution calling `process.exit(0)` bypassed every assertion** and was
   scored `pass:true`. *Fixed:* `process.exit` is trapped (throws) during the run, and correctness
   requires an all-assertions-ran `SENTINEL:` marker printed at the end — no sentinel (early exit /
   crash / timeout) ⇒ fail. Proof: `src/study-exec.test.ts` t-ex2.
2. **(major) `score-pilot.mjs` hard-coded `claimedDone:true`** — an agent timeout/empty response was
   indistinguishable from a false "done" claim. *Fixed:* `claimedDone` is derived from a non-empty
   solution (a non-response is a non-completion, not a false-done). Will matter at E3 scale.
3. **(minor) the `export`-stripping regex** corrupted any `"export "` inside a string. *Fixed:*
   strip only a line-start declaration (`/^(\s*)export\s+/gm`). Proof: t-ex4.

Re-scoring the pilot with the hardened scorer yields identical numbers (the attempts had neither an
early exit nor an empty response). `bash scripts/verify.sh` green; corpus 28/28 still valid.
