## Findings and resolutions

1. **(blocker) Hook check was an unscoped grep.** `grep guard-pretooluse.sh settings.json` passed
   even with `PreToolUse: []` as long as the filename appeared anywhere (e.g. under `PostToolUse`).
   The guard could be fully unwired from the one hook that intercepts tool calls and the heartbeat
   stayed green. *Fixed:* a jq check SCOPED to the `PreToolUse` array
   (`.hooks.PreToolUse[]?.hooks[]?.command | select(test("guard-pretooluse"))`). Proof: t-adr3b.

2. **(blocker) Gate check was a keyword grep.** `grep -E '_noop_check|no-op|refuse' verify.sh`
   passed on a `verify.sh` whose only match was a *comment*, with the real no-op refusal deleted.
   *Fixed:* a FUNCTIONAL check — run `LINT_CMD=true bash verify.sh` and require it to REFUSE (exit
   non-zero); the real gate refuses at its preflight (exit 3, fast, no suite run). Proof: t-adr4.

3. **(major) Guard probe was two hardcoded vectors.** Only `.env` + `rm -rf` were tested, so a guard
   narrowed to recognize exactly those (dropping `secrets/**`, `*.pem`, `dist/**`, `node_modules/**`,
   force-push, pipe-to-shell) scored healthy. *Fixed:* a representative spread of hard-protected
   paths + dangerous commands. Proof: t-adr2b (a guard blocking only .env+rm-rf now reds).

Minor: the two grep concerns above (same root); and plan.md's "files an issue when an adopter goes
inert" is broader than F5a's CI-RED signal — clarified in the ledger (auto-issue-filing needs the
API, deferred with F5b [needs-owner]).
